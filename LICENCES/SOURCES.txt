A link or a licence name does not replace a text: each one below is in this folder.

The runtime, the scheduler, the transports (CLI, MCP) and the scaffold files in app/.
Licence : MIT. Copyright (c) 2026 Cyberalien.
Text    : LICENCES/CyToolsCore-MIT.txt (the same notice is kept as
          app/LICENSE-CyToolsCore.txt, where the scaffold writes it).
Source  : https://github.com/MrMybal/CyToolsCore
Guides  : in that repository, README.md, Docs/CreatingYourFirstCyTool.md,
          Docs/Licensing.md and Docs/UpdateExistingProducts.md. The documents under
          app/Docs that describe the SDK are copies of its own.

CyUI (Cyberalien's Dear ImGui interface library), Python port
-------------------------------------------------------------
The look of the desktop interface: palette, themes, fonts and widgets.
Licence : MIT. Copyright (c) 2026 Cyberalien.
Text    : LICENCES/CyUI-MIT.txt.

Not distributed with the product
--------------------------------
The engine environments, the upstream sources they run and the model weights are fetched
by the application at installation, each under its own licence, several of them
non-commercial or subject to a personal acceptance. They are listed in
app/Docs/ThirdPartyNotices.md and shown in the Installation and Options tabs.
